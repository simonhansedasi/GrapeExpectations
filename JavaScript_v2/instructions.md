## Phase 1: Manuscript Format Check

Scientific Reports guidelines: nature.com/srep/author-instructions

- [ ] **Word count**: Scientific Reports Articles have no strict word limit — current
  length (~3,000 words main text) is well within range.
- [ ] **Abstract**: should be ≤ 200 words, unstructured. Check count.
- [ ] **Figures**: no strict limit. You have 4 main figures + S1 supplementary. Fine.
- [ ] **Figure files**: same as Nature family — 300 dpi minimum, TIFF or EPS preferred.
  Figures already regenerated at 300 dpi.
- [ ] **Supplementary**: upload as a single PDF (supplementary.tex → supplementary.pdf).

**Compile on Overleaf:**
1. Upload `paper.tex`, `references.bib`, `supplementary.tex`, and all figure files

## Phase 4: The Portal

Go to the Scientific Reports submission portal — find the link at nature.com/srep.

1. Create an account or log in
2. Select **"Submit New Manuscript"**
3. Article type: **"Article"**
4. Fill in: title, abstract (paste plain text), keywords (6 already in paper)
5. Add author: just you; enter ORCID 0009-0007-7635-8243
6. Upload files in the order the portal requests
7. **Suggested reviewers**: suggest 3–5 from your bibliography with no conflicts.
   Good candidates: Elith, Kearney, someone from the CMIP6 / agricultural
   adaptation community (e.g., Bunn, Jones).
8. **Excluded reviewers**: anyone with a conflict of interest
9. Review the portal-generated PDF proof before submitting
10. Submit

## Phase 5: After Submission

| Outcome | Timeline | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Desk rejection | 3–7 days | Try *Global Change Biology*, *Climatic Change*, or *Environmental Research Letters* |
| Sent to review | 1–2 weeks to hear | Wait; review typically 6–10 weeks at Scientific Reports |
| Decision | After review | Accept / Minor / Major / Reject |

Scientific Reports evaluates on scientific validity, not perceived impact — desk
rejection is less likely than at Nature Food.
